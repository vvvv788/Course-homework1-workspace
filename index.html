游戏功能GAME FEATURES

页面沿用同一套霓虹夜色配色、厚边框面板与像素按钮风格，让落地页与游戏画面保持一致氛围。 The page reuses the same neon-night palette, chunky bordered panels, and pixel buttons so the landing page matches the game's look and feel.

🧩

16 关递进16 Progressive Levels

前 8 关为经典推箱教学，后 8 关逐步引入道具与综合挑战，难度平滑上升。 Levels 1–8 teach classic Sokoban; levels 9–16 progressively add items and combine mechanics for a smooth difficulty curve.

🔧

四种道具Four Item Types

传送带、开关+门、彩色箱+同色目标、传送门，从第九关开始逐个登场。 Conveyors, switch & door, colored crates & matching targets, and portals — introduced one by one from level 9.

⭐

星级评价Star Ratings

依据 PAR 最优步数评定：达标三星、1.5 倍二星、通关即一星。 Rated against the PAR optimal step count: 3 stars at PAR, 2 stars at 1.5×PAR, 1 star for clearing.

↩️

撤销 / 重做 / 回放Undo / Redo / Replay

U 撤销、Y 重做、P 回放本关解法，R 随时重开，轻松试错不卡关。 U to undo, Y to redo, P to replay the solution, R to restart anytime — experiment freely without getting stuck.

游戏道具GAME ITEMS

四种道具让谜题从单纯推箱升级为空间与机关推理，是后八关的核心乐趣。 The four items turn plain pushing into spatial and mechanism puzzles — the core fun of the later eight levels.

➤

传送带Conveyor

带有方向箭头（^ v < >）的格子，箱子被推上去后会沿箭头方向自动滑动。 Tiles marked with an arrow (^ v < >); a crate pushed onto one slides automatically in that direction.

S / D

开关 + 门Switch + Door

玩家踩下开关，对应编号的门即永久开启，可打开原本封锁的通道。 Step on a switch and the matching door opens permanently, unlocking previously blocked paths.

🎨

彩色箱 + 同色目标Colored Crate + Target

红 / 蓝 / 绿箱子必须各自推到对应颜色的目标格，混色即无效。 Red / blue / green crates must each reach a target of the same color; mismatched colors do not count.

🌀

传送门Portal

成对出现的传送门，玩家或箱子进入其一，会从配对的另一个出来。 Paired portals: the player or a crate entering one exits from its twin.

怎么玩HOW TO PLAY

核心循环很简单：移动、推箱、用道具、让所有箱子归位。 The core loop is simple: move, push, use items, and get every crate home.

1

移动 Move

用方向键或 WASD 控制角色在地图中走动。 Use the arrow keys or WASD to walk around the map.

2

推箱 Push

走向箱子即可推动它，把每个箱子推到目标格上。 Walk into a crate to push it; park every crate on a target tile.

3

用道具 Use Items

善用传送带、开关、彩色目标与传送门破解更刁钻的布局。 Use conveyors, switches, colored targets, and portals to crack trickier layouts.

4

通关 Win

全部箱子归位即过关，系统按步数给出 1–3 星评价。 All crates home means you win; the game awards 1–3 stars by step count.
